# Latte with Lata - asset register

Every photo and the hero video on this site is a licensed placeholder sourced from Wikimedia Commons (search run 2026-09-14 through the Commons API). Nothing here comes from the two torn-down reference sites. All files were downloaded as Commons-rendered JPEG thumbnails (3000 px wide), then cropped to the contracted aspect ratio and re-encoded as JPEG (quality 0.86) in headless Chrome; the hero video (UPDATE-3, 2026-09-17) is our own 17.4 s edit of a CC BY-SA 4.0 Commons clip (see its row: cut, slowed, graded, looped).

Accepted licences: CC0, public domain, CC BY 2.0/3.0/4.0, CC BY-SA 2.0/3.0/4.0. Files rejected during research: anything else (CC BY-SA 2.5, FAL, "No restrictions"), PNG/TIFF, anything under 1000 px on the long side, brand-logo subjects (Starbucks / Illy / Compass cups), and close-up identifiable faces where the shot was clearly not a model/press photo.

**Licence mix:** 27 images CC0 (Unsplash-era uploads, no attribution legally required but credited anyway), 3 images CC BY (attribution required - strings below), 1 image CC BY-SA 4.0 (attribution + share-alike on the crop), video CC BY-SA 4.0 (UPDATE-3; was CC BY 2.0). Total payload at the time 10.4 MB (images 7.6 MB); the UPDATE-3 video is 0.84 MB (webm) + 2.34 MB (mp4 fallback).

## How to swap for the real cafe's photos (deployment)

1. Keep the filenames and aspect ratios exactly as contracted (see table): drop the replacement over `assets/images/<slot>.jpg` (and `assets/video/hero.webm`, plus a new `hero-poster.jpg` taken from the video's first clean frame). Portrait slots 2:3 at >= 1200x1800, food squares >= 1200x1200, episode/mosaic 3:2 >= 1600x1067, hero/visit 16:9 >= 1920x1080, strip 3:2 >= 2400x1600.
2. Update `content/assets-manifest.json`: `w`/`h` (builders emit them as `width`/`height` attributes), `alt` (describe the real photo), `author`/`license`/`src` (use `"license": "All rights reserved"` + the cafe as author for house photos).
3. Delete the corresponding row here (or the whole table once every slot is house-owned) and remove the credits page entry.
4. Re-run `verify/` validation (or `node <scratch>/validate.cjs`) - it checks that every contracted file exists, is a real JPEG/WebM and reports sizes; keep JPEGs under ~600 KB and the video under 12 MB (the hero video should ideally be re-encoded as 720p VP9 + an H.264 MP4 fallback, 6-8 s loop, no audio).
5. `episode-*.jpg` are meant to be replaced by real episode cover art from the podcast feed at build time; the six placeholders are only there so the rail has something to fan in.
