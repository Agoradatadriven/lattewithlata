What's on

Directions

  • On foot

    Five minutes from Harrowfield Market Square. Walk north up Bellwood Street and we are on the corner of Fenwick Lane, under the brown awning.

  • By bus or train

    Buses 4 and 11 stop at Fenwick Lane, right outside. Harrowfield Central station is a 12-minute walk.

  • By bike

    There are six bike stands on Fenwick Lane, beside the side door.

  • By car

    Street parking on Bellwood Street is free for 2 hours. The Market Square car park is five minutes away. There is one accessible bay outside the door.

Opening hours

Thursdays we reopen at 6:30 pm for the recording. Free, first come first seated, and the guest stays for a coffee afterwards.

Breakfast until 11:30 am. Brunch and lunch 11:30 am to 3:00 pm. Cakes, pastry and drinks until close.
