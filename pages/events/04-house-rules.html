In the room

HOUSE RULES

There are not many.

  1. 01

    Phones on silent

    The microphones hear everything. So does the person next to you.

  2. 02

    Applause is welcome

    So is laughing. It is a live room and it should sound like one.

  3. 03

    You may appear in photos

    We record audio and sometimes take photos for the show. We say so at the door. If you would rather stay out of frame, tell a member of staff and we will seat you accordingly.

  4. 04

    Be seated by 6:50 pm

    Reserved seats are released at 6:50 pm. Once the recording starts we seat latecomers at the back, between parts.

  5. 05

    Save your questions for afterwards

    There is no question time on the recording. The guest stays for a coffee, so ask them in person.
