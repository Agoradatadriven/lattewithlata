THE RECORDING NIGHT

Coffee. Leadership. Purpose. Real conversation. Thursday nights from 6:30 pm the chairs turn to face the corner table, and there is one for you.

Every episode of the podcast is recorded live in the cafe on a Thursday night, in front of whoever turns up. One guest, one conversation, four parts, with a short break halfway.

It is free. Seats are first come, first seated. If you want to be sure of a chair, reserve one online and we will hold it until 6:50 pm.

The kitchen runs a short list of plates until 8:30 pm and the counter stays open. When the recording ends the guest stays for a coffee, and so can you.
