Good to know

BEFORE YOU COME

Do I need a ticket?

No. It is free and you can walk in. If you want to be sure of a seat, reserve one online. Reserved seats are held until 6:50 pm.

Can I bring children?

Yes, if they can sit through an hour of grown-up conversation. The talk is for adults and sometimes covers illness, prison, loss and money.

Is the room accessible?

Entry is step-free and there is an accessible toilet. Tell us what you need when you reserve and we will hold a seat that works, including space for a wheelchair.

Will I be on the recording?

You will be heard clapping and laughing with everyone else. We never record audience conversations, and we say at the door when photos are being taken.
