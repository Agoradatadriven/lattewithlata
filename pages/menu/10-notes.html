BEFORE YOU ORDER

Allergens

Tell us about any allergy when you order, every time. One small kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs, sesame and fish, so we cannot promise that any dish is free of traces. GF means made with gluten-free ingredients, not made in a gluten-free kitchen.

Where it comes from

Coffee from a roaster two streets over. Bread, croissants and cakes baked here each morning. Eggs are free range. Vegetables come from the Saturday market when the season allows.

Prices at the counter

The prices are for a cup at the counter. A seat by the window, or at the corner table, costs the same. Prices include tax. There is no service charge, and tips are shared evenly across the team.

Large groups

We can seat up to 8 on one booking. For a bigger group, call or email us a few days ahead and we will plan the tables and, if you like, a set menu.
