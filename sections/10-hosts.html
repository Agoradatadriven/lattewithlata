Lata Singh Host

Lata Singh is the host. By day she is a senior operating leader in one of the country's largest safety-net healthcare networks, responsible for operations, quality, workforce and finance at a scale where every decision has a waiting room attached.

She is also pursuing doctoral study in leadership and innovation, which is why the questions here go one layer deeper than the highlight reel: what did it cost, who disagreed, and what would you do differently.

Latte with Lata is her personal platform, separate from her day job: unguarded conversations with the people building mission-driven organizations, because real leadership lessons come out over coffee, not in a boardroom.
